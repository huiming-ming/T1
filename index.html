Australian Household Energy Consumption

Welcome to the Appliance Energy Consumption portal. This site looks at how much power things like TVs, fridges and aircons actually use, so you can see where your bill is going.

Start with the Televisions page, which has a chart, a running cost estimate and a sample data table.

Reading an energy label

  • More stars means a more efficient appliance.
  • The kWh figure is the estimated energy use over one year.
  • Compare products of a similar size, not across sizes.
